01 — About

Beyond the résumé

What I actually spend my time building, breaking and reading about.

I'm an AI master's student who came here sideways — my bachelor's is in Petroleum Engineering, and I spent two and a half years in first-line application support at CGI before moving into machine learning full time. That route taught me something useful: I'm comfortable being the person who has to make a system work for real users, not just in a notebook.

Right now my research is in semi-supervised domain adaptation for medical image segmentation — getting a model trained on one hospital's data to hold up on another's without fresh expert annotations. Outside research I keep building LLM applications: retrieval pipelines that stay grounded in their sources, and agentic workflows that call real tools instead of guessing.

I like problems where the interesting part isn't the model architecture but everything around it — the data pipeline, the evaluation, the loss function that actually reflects what you care about, and whether the thing can be deployed at the end.
